Enterprise AI Solution Consulting Portfolio
An educational portfolio showing how I analyse business problems, design data and AI solutions, and communicate them to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
> **Disclaimer.** All companies, people, figures and datasets in this repository are fictional or synthetic and were created for learning. These are my own educational designs. They are not official implementations of any vendor's product (including Amnet Digital's publicly described offerings, which I use only as market reference) and do not describe real client work.
